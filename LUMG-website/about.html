Problem & target user

Problem statement

In neighbourhoods and homes everywhere, people over-consume and end up with unused items cluttering their space. They overbuy or outgrow things but have no easy way to pass them on. Current options fall short because recipients must be found and given time. As a result, usable items end up in landfills and harm the environment.

Target users

  • Neighbourhood residents and families with outgrown or unused items
  • People who want to donate or recycle but don't know where
  • Schools, charities and community groups that can reuse items

Project objectives

  • Make giving away, donating or recycling an item fast and easy
  • Show nearby donation and recycling locations
  • Use AI to suggest the right category and action
  • Raise awareness of overconsumption

Evidence

Our solution

LÜMG is a mobile app that guides people to share, donate or recycle unwanted items, and shows nearby locations that give those items a second life.

Purpose

Make responsible waste management easy, fast and accessible for everyone.

How it helps

Users post items in seconds, browse what neighbours are giving away, and find the nearest drop-off point on a map.

AI support

AI predicts the item's category and recommends the best action, so items aren't sent to an inappropriate place.

Built with

Flutter (Dart) for the mobile app and Supabase for database, login and image storage.
